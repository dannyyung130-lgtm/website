🔥 WEAPON TIPS

🎯

Know Your Range

Don't force every weapon into every situation. Choose your weapon based on the distance between you and your opponent.

⚡

Practice Your Aim

Good movement combined with accurate aim can make a bigger difference than simply choosing a popular weapon.

🧠

Build Your Loadout

Try pairing weapons that cover different ranges so you have an option when the fight changes.
